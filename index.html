About the numbers. This is a model built from approximate 2026 installed capacity by region (CEA), typical seasonal demand peaks and daily load shapes, and simple dispatch rules: must-run nuclear, renewables as available, hydro shaped to the evening peak, and coal balancing the rest with pithead plants in the east and west run hardest. Flows between regions come from a DC power-flow solve across the six main inter-regional corridors. It shows the patterns operators see, such as the north importing at night in summer, the midday solar bulge and the winter rabi peak in the west, but it is not real SCADA data.

Live mode. When this page is deployed with its data fetcher, it shows measured state demand for the current 15-minute block from Vidyut Pravah (Ministry of Power), summed into regions. The generation mix and each region's net position come from yesterday's Grid-India daily PSP report, shaped to the time of day. Flows between regions are then estimated with the same power-flow solve, so they are estimates, not metered values.
